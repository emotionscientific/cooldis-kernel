# Code of Conduct

Verlet is a technical project, but the work is done by people. Keep discussion
direct, generous, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume good faith until there is evidence otherwise.
- Critique ideas, code, and behavior; do not attack people.
- Make room for maintainers to say no, ask for scope cuts, or defer work.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Respect security, privacy, and responsible disclosure boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Posting private information, secrets, credentials, or exploit details in
  public channels.
- Spam, automated drive-by contributions, or generated noise that burdens
  maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or delete comments; close issues or pull
requests; block users; or restrict interaction when needed to protect the
project. Security reports should follow `SECURITY.md`.
